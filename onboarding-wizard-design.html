Step 4 of 8

Generate a permanent access token

This keeps your hospital connected without needing to reconnect every day. It only takes a minute inside Meta's Business Settings.

1

Open Business Settings and go to Users → System Users.

Open Meta Business Settings ↗
2

Create a system user and assign it to your WhatsApp app, then generate a token with messaging permissions.

This token won't expire every 24 hours like the default one — that's what makes it permanent.

You'll find this on the System User's "Generate token" screen.
